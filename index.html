📱 Background App Guide

This website cannot inspect all installed Android apps or identify which apps are running in the background.

How to check your Android phone

1. Open Settings on your phone.
2. Select Battery or Battery Usage.
3. Review battery consumption by app.
4. Select an app to see available background activity details.
5. Adjust background restrictions if necessary.

Menu names differ between Android devices. Use your phone's built-in battery settings for accurate app usage information.

💡 Power Saving Tips

• Reduce screen brightness.
• Enable Battery Saver when necessary.
• Review apps with high battery consumption.
• Avoid excessive heat.
• Turn off unused connections when appropriate.
• Keep your phone software updated.
